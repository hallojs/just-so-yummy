# Ingredients
- 500g pasta
- 400g beans
- 400g potatoes
- 1/2 portion [Pesto alla Genovese]({{<relref "pesto_alla_genovese.en.md">}})

# Instructions
1. First, prepare half a portion of Pesto alla Genovese.
2. Then wash and halve the beans. Peel the potatoes and cut into bite-sized pieces.
3. Next, cook the potatoes, beans and pasta in a large pot of salted water for 10-14, 10-12, and 10 minutes respectively.
4. Finally, fold in pesto to taste.

Enjoy your meal!
