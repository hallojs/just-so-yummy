Feta cheese spread for baguette or as a dip for grilling.

# Ingredients
- 100g feta cheese
- 200g cream cheese
- 100g sour cream
- 2 tbsp ajvar
- One clove of garlic
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley

# Instructions
1. Peel and finely chop the garlic clove.
2. Mix feta cheese, cream cheese, sour cream and garlic.
3. Stir in parsley and ajvar.

Enjoy your meal!
