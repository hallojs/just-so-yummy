# Ingredients {{< portionen base=2 unit="Baguettes" >}}
- 300ml lukewarm water
- 15g fresh yeast
- 5g sugar
- 380g wheat flour (type 550)
- 7g salt
- 125g crispy fried onions (optional)

# Instructions
1. Add water, yeast and sugar to a bowl and mix well.
2. Add flour, salt and fried onions and knead into a smooth dough.
3. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rise for 2 hours.
4. Divide the dough into two equal pieces, shape into baguettes and place on a baguette tray with baking paper.
5. Dust the baguettes with flour and bake for about 25 minutes at 240°C with top and bottom heat.

Enjoy your meal!
