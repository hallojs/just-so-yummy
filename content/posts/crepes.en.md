This makes enough for about 3 hungry people.

# Ingredients
- 4 eggs
- 500ml milk
- 1 pinch of salt
- 2 tbsp oil
- 250g flour
    - Either 250g wheat flour (type 405),
    - 250g spelt flour (type 630),
    - or 200g of one of the above flours and 50g whole wheat flour
- Butter

# Instructions
1. Whisk the first four ingredients together well in a bowl.
2. Stir in the flour.
3. Fry the crêpes with butter in a pan.

Enjoy your meal!

*PS: The pan is still hot... cut an apple into small wedges and toss with vanilla sugar (and cinnamon) in butter.*
