    Spring Salad

    This makes a medium-sized bowl.

    Ingredients

    • 100g lamb’s lettuce
    • 2 oranges
    • 2 shallots
    • 2 tbsp red wine
    • 2 tbsp pine nuts
    • 2 tbsp maple syrup
    • 2 tbsp olive oil
    • Salt
    • Pepper

    Instructions

    1. Wash the lamb’s lettuce and place in a large bowl.
    2. Toast the pine nuts in a pan on low to medium heat until lightly browned.
    3. Peel the shallots and cut into fine cubes.
    4. Peel the oranges, remove white membranes and cut into finger-width pieces.
    5. Mix red wine, maple syrup, olive oil, salt and pepper into a dressing.
    6. Add shallots and oranges to the lamb’s lettuce and fold in the dressing.

    Enjoy your meal!
