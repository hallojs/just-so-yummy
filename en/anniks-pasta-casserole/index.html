    Annik's Pasta Casserole

    This makes enough for a medium to large casserole dish.

    Ingredients

    • 300g pasta
    • 200g grated cheese
    • 150g cooked ham
    • 3 eggs
    • 40g crème fraîche
    • Salt
    • Nutmeg
    • Parmesan

    Instructions

    1. Cook the pasta and cut the ham into small rectangles.
    2. Layer the pasta alternately with the ham and cheese in a casserole dish.
    3. Mix the eggs with the crème fraîche, a pinch of salt and nutmeg, then pour over the pasta.
    4. Sprinkle the Parmesan on top and bake at 200°C for about 15-20 minutes.

    Enjoy your meal!
