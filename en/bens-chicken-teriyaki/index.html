    Ben's Chicken Teriyaki

    This recipe serves 4 people and is typically served with rice.

    Ingredients for the Sauce

    • 3 tbsp soy sauce
    • 3 tbsp brown sugar
    • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
    • 1 tbsp sesame oil
    • 1 tsp grated ginger
    • 1 tsp grated garlic

    Other Ingredients

    • 450g chicken breast fillet
    • 1/4 tsp salt
    • 1/4 tsp pepper
    • 1.5 tbsp oil
    • 1 tsp sesame seeds
    • One spring onion

    Instructions

    1. Grate ginger and garlic and mix with the other sauce ingredients in a bowl.
    2. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
    3. Heat the oil in a pan and fry the chicken. Season with salt and pepper.
    4. Fry the meat on high heat for 5-6 minutes until the water has completely evaporated and it’s nicely browned.
    5. Add the teriyaki sauce and let it simmer for 3-4 minutes on medium heat until the sauce has thickened.
    6. Garnish with sesame seeds and chopped spring onions.

    Enjoy your meal!
